Leverage, trading instruments, commission fees, and supported platforms

⚡ Leverage by Account Type

Leverage allows traders to control larger positions with less capital. Different account types have different leverage limits based on risk profile.

Account TypeLeverageNotes
Challenge (Phase 1 & 2)1:100Full leverage during evaluation
Instant Funding — 510101:100Standard leverage
Instant Funding — 510zero1:33Lower leverage, more conservative
Funded (Challenge graduates)1:10Reduced leverage on funded account
💡
Why 510zero has Lower Leverage?

The 510zero plan is designed for traders who prefer frequent withdrawals without profit targets. Lower leverage (1:33) encourages more conservative position sizing, balancing the flexibility of no profit targets with reduced risk exposure.

Commission Impact on Profit Calculation

Commissions are part of trading costs and are factored into profit/loss calculations. Sales partners should help clients understand total cost of trading including spreads + commissions.

💻 Trading Platforms

📱
MetaTrader 5 (MT5)

Industry-standard platform. Available on Desktop (Windows/Mac), Web, iOS, and Android. Full EA support, advanced charting, multi-timeframe analysis.

🖥️
MatchTrader

Modern web-based platform. Clean UI, fast execution, perfect for prop traders. Accessible via browser without installation. Mobile-friendly.

⚙️
cTrader

Advanced trading platform with Level II pricing, fast order execution, and sophisticated algorithmic trading support (cBots). Available on Desktop, Web, iOS, and Android.

✅
Platform Selection Tip for Clients

Recommend MT5 for clients who use automated trading (EAs) or need advanced technical analysis. Recommend MatchTrader for clients who prefer simplicity and web-based access without downloads. Recommend cTrader for clients who want advanced order types and algorithmic trading with cBots.
