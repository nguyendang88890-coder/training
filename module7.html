🪪 KYC (Know Your Customer) Requirements

✅
KYC is Mandatory for All Clients

Every trader must complete KYC verification before they can withdraw profits. Encourage clients to complete KYC early to avoid delays when requesting payouts.

KYC Requirements

  • Legal age requirement — Clients must be of legal age (18+) in their country of residence
  • Government-issued ID — Passport, national ID, or driver's license
  • Proof of address — Utility bill or bank statement (not older than 3 months)
  • KYC must be completed before the first payout request
  • Some countries may require additional documentation

🚫 Prohibited Trading Practices

Certain trading strategies are strictly prohibited because they exploit platform vulnerabilities rather than demonstrating real trading skill.

⛔
Prohibited Practices — Escalating Consequences

Violations are handled on a first/second-offence basis. Some practices result in immediate termination; others follow a warning-then-terminate structure. See the table in Module 2 for the full breakdown.

Prohibited PracticeDescription1st Violation2nd Violation
Tick Scalping Abnormal frequency of scalping trades exploiting quote latency If account profitable: refuse payout + reset, PS → 25% next. If at loss: no action taken Terminate
Cross-Account Hedging Opposing positions across 2+ WMT accounts or vs other prop firms Reset; PS → 10% on next payout Terminate
Arbitrage Exploiting price differences between brokers/feeds Immediate termination

Other Prohibited Activities

  • Using any third-party service that guarantees passing the challenge
  • Account sharing or allowing others to trade the funded account
  • Copy trading from an external signal provider designed to exploit the evaluation
  • Coordinated group trading to manipulate evaluation results

🌏 Indonesia & Thailand — Specific Thresholds

ℹ️
Treated the Same as Other Countries — Different Thresholds Only

Clients from Indonesia and Thailand are subject to the same violation consequences as all other clients. The key difference is that certain violation thresholds are adjusted for these regions (e.g., profit withdrawal thresholds for the 510Zero plan).

  • Indonesia and Thailand clients follow the same 1st/2nd violation structure as all clients
  • Specific thresholds that differ: 510Zero profit share reduction applies at <8% profit (vs <6% for other regions)
  • When onboarding clients from these regions, clearly explain the applicable thresholds

✅ Compliance Best Practices for Sales Partners

💼
Your Role in Compliance

As a sales partner, you are the first point of contact for clients. Proper client education about rules protects both the client's investment and your commission.

  • Always verify country eligibility before helping a client sign up
  • Communicate the refund policy clearly before purchase
  • Explain prohibited practices — don't assume clients know
  • Emphasize KYC — early verification prevents payout delays
  • Indonesia/Thailand clients — explain the specific thresholds that apply to their region
  • Never promise guaranteed profits or specific results
  • Report suspicious behavior to the compliance team immediately
