Introducing Partner

Commission structures, partner tiers, cookie tracking, and payout rules

🤝 Commission Overview

WeMasterTrade operates an introducing partner program with generous commissions and lifetime tracking. As a sales partner, you earn commissions on referrals.

⭐
25% First Transaction Commission

Introducing partners earn 25% commission on the FIRST purchase by each referred client. Commissions are paid for the lifetime of the customer.

🍪 Cookie Tracking & Attribution

ℹ️
30-Day Cookie Window

When a user clicks your IP link, a cookie is stored for 30 days. This means if a user clicks your IP link but later visits the website directly to make a purchase — without clicking your link again — you still receive credit and earn the commission.

How Attribution Works

  • Last-click attribution: The introducing partner whose link was clicked last (within 30 days) gets credit
  • Cookie persists for 30 days from the click
  • If a client clears cookies before purchasing, the attribution may be lost
  • Commissions are tracked in your IP dashboard in real-time

🎟️ IP Referral Discount Code

🏷️
Exclusive Discount for New Clients

Each IP can request a personal referral discount code to share with potential clients. The discount ranges from 20% to 30% off their first purchase.

Eligibility Conditions

  • The discount code is only valid for customers who register a new WeMasterTrade account
  • The customer must not have made any previous purchase on the platform
  • Each code is tied to the requesting IP — conversions are still tracked and attributed accordingly
📌
How to Request

IPs can submit a referral discount code request directly through the Introducing Partner tab. The approved discount rate (20–30%) is assigned by WeMasterTrade based on the IP's tier and performance.

💸 Commission Payout Rules

Minimum Withdrawal Thresholds

RegionMinimum Balance to Withdraw
All Regions$100
⏰
31-Day Holding Period

Commissions become eligible for withdrawal 31 days after the client's payment. This accounts for potential refund windows and chargebacks.

Important Payout Notes

  • Commissions are calculated on the net amount paid by the client
  • If a client gets a refund (before trading starts), your commission is reversed
  • Once a client starts trading, no refunds are issued, so your commission is secured
  • Withdrawals are processed via the available payment methods in your region
