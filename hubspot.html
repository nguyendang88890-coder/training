Purpose & Scope

This guide helps WeGolden Customer Support staff work in HubSpot: look up customers, handle chats and tickets in Help Desk, track tickets through pipelines, and view reports.

System: HubSpot (app-na2.hubspot.com)  |  Scope: Home, Contacts, Deals, Help Desk, Tickets, Reports/Dashboards, Settings

📖 Step-by-Step Instructions

💡
Daily CS work centres on Contacts, Help Desk and Tickets. The rule: take the request, help the customer, then close the ticket.

1 — Find a Customer & Read the Contact Record

The Find in HubSpot search bar at the top is the tool you will use most. Type the customer's name or email to open their record.

Right column — TicketsAll of the customer's tickets: live chat, email, requests to OperationVery important: review past requests and their progress
Right column — Companies/CampaignsCompany, marketing campaignsNot used — ignore

2 — Receive & Reply to Customers in Help Desk

Help Desk (Service › Help Desk) is where you reply to customer emails, live chats and tickets. The icon on each conversation shows the channel: live chat or email.

  • Email: There is currently only one address, support@wegolden.com. Every email sent there lands in Help Desk.
  • Live chat: Customers chat from the WeGolden website. An AI chat answers simple questions first. When the customer asks for a human, the AI collects their email and hands the conversation to the support team.

Steps when a customer needs a human:

  1. The ticket is assigned to a Ticket owner. The owner gets a "You have been assigned…" notification under the bell icon.
  2. Click the notification to open the ticket directly.
  3. Greet and help the customer in the Live Chat box, then click Send.
  4. When the customer has no more questions, close the ticket.

3 — Ticket Owner & Assigning Tickets

  • By design: A workflow assigns tickets automatically to the regional team based on the customer's country (e.g. a customer from Turkey goes to the Turkey team). Local teams do not need to handle unassigned tickets.
  • Unassigned tickets (country unknown): The Headquarters team handles them first. Once the country is known, Headquarters reassigns the ticket to the right local team.
  • When the workflow fails: Open the ticket, click Ticket owner in the right column, and choose a person or team (Global Team, Philippines, Egypt, India…).

4 — Close the Ticket When Done

CS must monitor HubSpot throughout the shift to catch new emails and live chats. Every request must end with the ticket closed; this applies to all agents.

ChannelPipelineClosing Status
Live chatHelpdesk ChatClosed (click End chat, then change the status)

"Contact us" formSupport PipelineClosed
⚠️
Closed tickets disappear from the default view. To see them again, use a custom view.

5 — "Contact Us" Form on the Website

When a customer submits the Contact us form, HubSpot creates a ticket in Support Pipeline with status New. The form contents (name, email, message) appear in the conversation.

This channel is rare but more formal than email. Reply as you would to an email, wait for the customer's response, then move the ticket to Closed.

6 — Create a View to Track Tickets

A view is a saved set of filters. Create one that includes every status so you can also see closed tickets.

  1. Click the + next to Views.
  2. Enter a name, choose sharing as Private, Team(s) or Everyone, then click Next.
  3. Add filters — for example Ticket status is any of [all statuses]. Click Save.
  4. To edit a view: hover over its name, click the three dots and choose Edit view.

7 — Project Task Pipeline: Asking the Operation Team to Check

Project Task is an internal pipeline, not directly visible to customers. CS uses it to ask the Operation team to check something in the backend.

⚠️
CS Local can only view Project Task tickets — not create or move them. CS Global creates and manages these tickets.

How to create one (CS Global only):

  1. Open the customer's contact record → in the Tickets block → click + Add > Create new.
  2. Ticket name: the request (e.g. "Please check this account if it violation or not").
  3. Pipeline: Project Task | Ticket status: TO DO.
  4. Ticket description: Account ID and the details to check. Click Create.

8 — Reports & Dashboards

Go to More › Reporting for Reports and Dashboards. Example: the Withdrawal Data Analytics dashboard tracks withdrawal counts and speed.

  • HubSpot only holds deposit and withdrawal data. The Contact page shows each customer's transactions only; a regional total needs a dedicated dashboard.
  • Volume and P&L are NOT in HubSpot — use the Admin panel or Lightdash (which pulls data from MT5 and the Admin panel).
  • CS Local can most likely view dashboards but not create them.
  • Need a specific dashboard or data: send a request to Vincent, David or Aiden stating exactly what data you need.

9 — Other Items

  • Marketing, Content, Sales and Revenue under More belong to other departments. CS rarely uses them.
  • The Call button on a customer record cannot place calls yet. The company uses CallHippo; integration is pending Aiden's check.

10 — Filter Deals by Type (Withdrawal / Deposit)

Withdrawals and deposits are kept in separate deal pipelines, so you filter by type by switching the pipeline.

  1. Click Deals in the left menu and open the All deals view.
  2. Click the pipeline dropdown on the right of the filter bar.
  3. Choose the pipeline you need:
OptionShows
All PipelinesEvery deal: deposits, withdrawals and internal transfers
#ID_out / WithdrawWithdrawal deals only
DepositDeposit deals only
All (pipeline name)A separate pipeline named "All" — currently holds no deals

Narrow further with Deal owner, Create date, Last activity date or Close date on the filter bar. Click Filter for advanced options. To reuse a filter, save it as a new view (e.g. "Withdrawals").

11 — Filter and Tell Contacts Apart

Filter contacts:

  • Click Contacts → choose a view (e.g. All contacts or Unassigned contacts).
  • Use the filter bar: Contact owner, Create date, Last activity date, Lead status. Click Advanced filters for Country/Region and other properties.
  • Switch to board view to group contacts by Lifecycle stage: Subscriber → Lead → Marketing Qualified Lead → Sales Qualified Lead → Opportunity → Customer. Most contacts sit in Lead or Opportunity.

Tell contacts apart — several customers can share the same name. Before replying or creating a ticket, confirm the right person using:

  • Email — the most reliable identifier, shown under the name.
  • Country/Region and phone number — in Key information.
  • Deals — check the accounts match what the customer mentions.
  • History — the Activities tab shows earlier chats and emails.

12 — View Account Details

In HubSpot, each trading account appears as a deal. Open it in one of two ways:

  • From the customer's contact record: in the Deals block → click the deal.
  • From Deals: search for the account number (e.g. Acc 7888).

13 — Send Email & Set Up an Email Signature

Send an email:

  • Reply to a support request: in Help Desk, open the ticket → write in the Email box at the bottom → click Send. The email goes out from support@wegolden.com and stays in the ticket thread.
  • Email a customer directly: open their contact record → click Email in the left column → enter subject and message → click Send. Logged in the Activities tab.

Set up your personal signature:

  1. Click the Settings gear in the top bar.
  2. In the left sidebar, go to General → open the Email tab.
  3. In the Configure section, click Edit email signature (or Manage email signatures on some plans).
  4. Type your signature or switch to HTML to paste a designed signature.
  5. Click Save.
⚠️
Team signature for support@wegolden.com is managed under Settings › Inbox & Help Desk › Help Desk › Channels. This needs admin rights — ask your team lead if it needs changing.

14 — Monitor the AI HubSpot Agent

The AI agent ("WeGolden's Customer Support Agent") answers website live chats first. CS should check its conversations daily to catch wrong answers and chats it failed to hand over.

Daily check in Help Desk:

  • Open the Customer Agent view to see conversations the AI is handling (status: AI chat).
  • Read the AI's replies. The line "Unassigned from WeGolden's Customer Support Agent" marks where the AI handed over to a human.
  • If the AI gave a wrong or incomplete answer: take over the chat, correct the information, and report the case to your team lead so the AI's knowledge can be updated.

Performance reports — go to More › Service › Customer Agent › Analyze › Reports:

15 — AP/IB Telegram Support Groups

Each Telegram support group for an AP/IB partner is tracked as a record in the Project Task object, under the AP SUPPORT pipeline.

  1. Go to More › CRM › Project Task (or search "Project Task" in Find in HubSpot).
  2. In the pipeline dropdown, choose AP SUPPORT. Switch to board view to see stages as columns.
  3. Records are named SP_partner name_WGD (e.g. SP_HoangMin_WGD). Use Search to find a partner by name.

📝 Working Notes

  • Monitor HubSpot continuously during your shift — this is daily work, not something to check now and then.
  • Close the ticket as soon as the request is handled: Closed for live chat, Resolved for email.
  • Read the history in the middle column before replying — don't ask again for what the customer already said.
  • On the Project Task board, CS only works in NEEDS INFORMATION, WAITING CLIENT RESPONSE and RESULTS. Never drag tickets into Operation, Risk or Super Admin stages.
  • Record all communication with the Operation team in the ticket's Note, not outside HubSpot.
  • Put the Account ID and details in the Ticket description, not only in the ticket name.
