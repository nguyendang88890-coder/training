📈 Profit Consistency Rule (PCR)

The Profit Consistency Rule ensures traders demonstrate consistent, professional trading behavior rather than relying on a single lucky day.

📐
Formula: Best Day ÷ Total Profit × 100 ≤ 20%

A single trading day cannot contribute more than 20% of the total profit earned during the evaluation.

When It Applies

  • Challenge Phase 1 — PCR is enforced (30% threshold)
  • 51010 Instant Funding — PCR applies
  • 510Zero — No profit target, PCR applied
  • Challenge Funded account — PCR is NOT enforced

Worked Example

🧮
Example Calculation

Total profit earned: $6,000
Best single day profit: $1,000
PCR = $1,000 ÷ $6,000 × 100 = 16.7% ✓ (within 20% limit — full payout)

If best day was $1,500 (PCR = 25%, between 20–30%):
→ Profit share reduced to 10% for accounts ≤ $100K
→ Accounts above $100K: not qualified for payout

If best day was $2,100 (PCR = 35%, above 30%):
→ Not qualified for payout (all account sizes)

⚠️ Risk Consistency Rule (RCR)

The Risk Consistency Rule limits the maximum risk exposure per trade idea to protect capital and ensure professional risk management.

Account TypeMax Risk Per Trade Idea
51010 (Instant Funded)2% of initial account balance
510Zero (Instant Funded)2% of initial account balance
Funded Account (NOPC)1% of initial account balance
⚠️
Trade Idea Definition

A "trade idea" refers to orders on the same symbol, in the same direction, and closed at the same time. The combined risk of all such orders counts toward the RCR limit. Correlated positions on different symbols or accounts are assessed separately and do not combine into a single trade idea.

RCR is calculated on the initial account balance and includes floating P&L, swap, commissions, and fees in the risk exposure.

📢
RCR & Challenge Package

The Risk Consistency Rule does not apply to Challenge Phase 1. It applies to the Challenge Funded account at 1% (immediate termination, no reset/reactivation). RCR also remains in effect for 51010, 510Zero, and NOPC Instant Funding accounts.

🛑 Max Loss Limit (MLL)

📊
10% of Initial Account Balance

The account equity (or balance including open positions) must never fall below 90% of the initial account balance. This is a balance-based calculation.

How It's Calculated

  • Based on the initial account balance (not current balance)
  • Includes open floating positions in the calculation
  • If account = $100,000, then equity must stay above $90,000
  • Breaching this limit results in immediate account closure
💡
Example

Account size: $50,000 | Max Loss = 10% = $5,000
Account cannot drop below: $50,000 - $5,000 = $45,000 (including open P&L)

📅 Daily Loss Limit (DLL)

📉
5% Daily Loss Limit

Calculated from the higher of: Starting day's equity OR starting day's balance. Ensures that each day has its own risk ceiling.

Definition

The Maximum Daily Loss uses the higher value between starting day's equity and starting day's balance as the baseline. The day's equity includes:

  • Floating P&L (open positions)
  • All closed positions for that day
  • Swap, commission, and fees are all included in the calculation

The Daily Loss Limit is calculated as a percentage of the initial account balance.

What Happens If I Break the Daily Loss Rule?

🚨
Account Auto-Liquidated — Rule Violation

If you hit the Daily Loss Limit, your account is automatically liquidated for the remainder of the trading day. This is a rule violation — you CANNOT resume trading. You will need to purchase a new package to start again.

⚠️
Auto-Liquidation Mechanism

The auto-liquidation system sends a market order to close open positions. Due to market fluctuation at the time of execution, the final P&L may end up exceeding the daily loss limit slightly.

⏱️ Trading Interval Restriction

The Trading Interval Rule has two components: an open-position restriction and a post-close wait.

🚫
Interval Open: Active Losing Position Block

If you have an open losing position on a symbol, you cannot open a new position on the same symbol on any of your other accounts (including Tournament accounts), regardless of time elapsed.

⏳
Interval Close: 1-Hour Wait After Closing a Loss

After closing a losing position on a specific symbol, you must wait at least 1 hour before opening a new position on that same symbol on your other accounts.

  • Applies across all accounts owned by the same trader (including Tournament accounts)
  • Only triggered by losing positions — winning trades don't activate either restriction
  • Symbol-specific: a losing EUR/USD position only restricts EUR/USD on other accounts
  • Designed to prevent emotional revenge trading and cross-account manipulation
⚠️
Violation Consequences
  • 1st violation: Account reset or reactivation; profit share reduced to 25% on the next payout
  • 2nd violation: Account terminated

⚡ Tick Scalping

Tick scalping — opening and closing positions within seconds to exploit quote latency — is a restricted practice, not an outright prohibition. Casual scalping strategies are permitted.

📋
When It Becomes an Issue

If the Risk Team determines that all or most of a trader's activity consists of scalping with abnormal frequency or unusual regularity, the account will be reviewed. See Module 7 for violation consequences.

🔄 Hedging Rules

✅
Hedging Within a Single Account: Allowed

Traders may place opposing positions within the same account as a legitimate risk management strategy.

🚫
Cross-Account Hedging: Prohibited

Hedging between two or more WMT accounts (including accounts under the same profile) or against positions at other prop firms is strictly prohibited.

⚠️
Hedge Stacking: Prohibited

Placing a continuous series of opposing positions solely for the purpose of risk control within a single account — rather than as a legitimate trade idea — is also prohibited (per WMT FAQ).

⚠️
Violation Consequences
  • 1st violation: Account reset; profit share reduced to 10% on the next payout
  • 2nd violation: Account terminated

💻 Device Sharing Policy

✅
Multiple Personal Devices: Allowed

Traders may use multiple devices that belong to them to access their accounts (e.g., personal laptop, home desktop, mobile).

🚫
Shared Devices: Prohibited

Using a device that does not belong to you, or sharing a device with another trader — regardless of your relationship — is prohibited (per WMT FAQ).

🚫 Profit Consistency Manipulation

Intentionally manipulating trading activity to control the Profit Consistency ratio is prohibited. Examples include (per WMT FAQ):

  • Splitting trade closures to shift profits into future periods
  • Closing the majority of positions while leaving a small portion open to close later
  • Placing unusual losing orders the day after a large profit day to reduce the PCR ratio
⛔
These are Prohibited Activities

If the Risk Team identifies intentional PC manipulation, the account may be terminated. Trade naturally — do not engineer your Profit Consistency ratio.
