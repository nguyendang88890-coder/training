Affiliate & Partner Policies

Commission structures, partner tiers, cookie tracking, and payout rules

🤝 Commission Overview

WeMasterTrade operates a 3-level affiliate system with generous commissions and lifetime tracking. As an affiliate partner, you earn commissions on referrals and sub-referrals.

⭐
25% First Transaction Commission

Level 1 affiliates earn 25% commission on the FIRST purchase by each referred client, plus tiered upsell commissions based on monthly volume. Commissions are paid for the lifetime of the customer.

3-Level Commission Structure

🍪 Cookie Tracking & Attribution

ℹ️
30-Day Cookie Window

When a user clicks your affiliate link, a cookie is stored for 30 days. This means if a user clicks your affiliate link but later visits the website directly to make a purchase — without clicking your link again — you still receive credit and earn the commission.

How Attribution Works

  • Last-click attribution: The affiliate whose link was clicked last (within 30 days) gets credit
  • Cookie persists for 30 days from the click
  • If a client clears cookies before purchasing, the attribution may be lost
  • Commissions are tracked in your affiliate dashboard in real-time

⏰
31-Day Holding Period

Commissions become eligible for withdrawal 31 days after the client's payment. This accounts for potential refund windows and chargebacks.

Important Payout Notes

  • Commissions are calculated on the net amount paid by the client
  • If a client gets a refund (before trading starts), your commission is reversed
  • Once a client starts trading, no refunds are issued, so your commission is secured
  • Withdrawals are processed via the available payment methods in your region
