📦 1.1 Challenge Package

The Challenge Package is a 1-phase evaluation program designed to assess a trader's skills before granting funded status. There is no time limit — traders can take as long as they need. Available through Customize Package or Trading Capital. Supported platform: MT5.

⭐
Key Benefits

Free Swap  ·  Overnight & Weekend Holding  ·  News Trading  ·  90% profit share on Funded  ·  Payout on demand (every 7 days)

Important Notes

Payout — Funded Account
  • Payout available on demand once all trading objectives are met.
  • Payout frequency: every 7 days from the previous payout.
  • Processing time: within 24 hours.
  • Profit sharing is always 90% — no profit target required to withdraw on Funded.

⚡ 1.2 Instant Funding

Instant Funding gives traders immediate access to a live trading account — no evaluation phase required. Perfect for experienced traders who want to start trading right away. All plans support Scale-up to $1,000,000 and cost starting from $70.

💻
Available on MT5 & cTrader

All WeMasterTrade plans are available on both MetaTrader 5 (MT5) and cTrader with identical conditions. Clients choose their preferred platform at the time of purchase.

510Zero Plan — Pricing

No profit target — withdraw anytime. First reward after 1 day. Profit share depends on withdrawal amount relative to threshold.

📋
510Zero — Important Rules
  • Profit Consistency (PC) applies — all 510Zero accounts are subject to the Profit Consistency Rule
  • Minimum 5 trading days after each payout — traders must trade at least 5 more days before the next withdrawal
ℹ️
510Zero Profit Share Rule:
If profit withdrawn is less than 6% (8% for Indonesia & Thailand customers) → flat 10% profit share.
If profit withdrawn is 6% or more → tiered share: 1st 50% · 2nd 75% · 3rd 90%.
