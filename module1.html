Understand all product offerings: Challenge Package and Instant Funding

📦 1.1 Challenge Package

The Challenge Package is a two-phase evaluation program designed to assess a trader's skills before granting funded status. There is no time limit — traders can take as long as they need. Account sizes range from $10,000 to $100,000.

⭐
Key Benefits

Free Swap on all phases  ·  90% profit share on Funded  ·  30% reset discount  ·  One-time fee refunded after withdrawing 6% cumulative profit on Funded

📋
Important Notes
  • After passing both phases, the customer receives the Funded account.
  • Once a customer withdraws 6% cumulative profit from the Funded account, the one-time fee is refunded and they receive 30% profit share from Phase 2.
  • Phase 1 & Phase 2 customers do not need to sign an E-contract. E-contract is only required for the Funded phase.
  • Customers who fail Phase 1 or 2 may repurchase the same package value with a 30% discount (one time).
💰
Payout Phase — Funded Account
  • Weekly payouts available with no minimum withdrawal amount.
  • Profit sharing is always 90% — no profit target required to withdraw.
  • After reaching 6% cumulative profit target on the Funded account: customers receive the 30% Phase 2 profit share + a full refund of the package purchase fee.
