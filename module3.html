Profit Sharing & Payouts

How traders earn profit splits, payout progression, and certificate rewards

📈 Payout Progression (Challenge & 51010)

Traders earn increasing profit splits as they demonstrate consistent performance. This rewards reliable, long-term traders.

🏆
Progressive Profit Share — Up to 90%

Traders don't start at 90% — they earn it through consistent performance over multiple payout cycles.

Payout NumberTrader's ShareWMT's Share
1st Payout50%50%
2nd Payout75%25%
3rd Payout onwards90%10%
💡
Why Progressive?

The progressive payout system ensures that only consistently profitable traders reach the 90% tier. This aligns incentives between WeMasterTrade and its funded traders.

⚡
24-Hour Payout SLA — Effective March 12, 2026

WeMasterTrade guarantees payout processing within 24 hours of an approved withdrawal request. This applies to all Challenge and Instant Funding payout requests.

🎯 510Zero Profit Sharing

The 510Zero package uses a performance-based reward system. Traders earn a profit share based on their trading performance level, with no minimum profit target required — simply request a withdrawal whenever ready.

⚠️
Please note that for the 510Zero packages:

If the profit is less than 6%, the profit share of the 510Zero package will be reduced to a fixed 10% (under 8% profit, profit share reduce to 10% for Indonesia and Thailand customers).

  • Performance-based reward applies — higher profit level earns a higher profit share
  • If profit < 6%: profit share is reduced to a fixed 10%
  • Under 8% profit: profit share is also reduced to 10% for Indonesia and Thailand customers
  • No minimum profit target — withdrawal can be requested at any time

🚫 Refund Policy

❌
NO REFUNDS Once Trading Begins

Challenge fees and Instant Funding fees are strictly non-refundable once the trader has placed even a single trade. This policy is absolute and must be communicated clearly before clients purchase.

Refund Eligibility (Pre-Trading Only)

  • A refund may be considered if the trader has NOT yet started trading
  • Once the first trade is placed, no refund under any circumstances
  • Technical issues that prevented trading may be reviewed case by case
  • Refund requests must be submitted through official support channels
✅
Sales Partner Responsibility

Always inform clients about the no-refund policy BEFORE purchase. Explain it clearly so they understand the commitment. This prevents disputes and protects both parties.
